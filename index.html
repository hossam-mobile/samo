About Us

SAMO is a software development studio founded by two specialists who believe great products come from small teams that care.

We're a Mobile Developer and a UI/UX Designer. Between us, we've contributed to production products across e-commerce, delivery, healthcare, education, and business management — through roles at other companies and teams.

We started SAMO to work directly with businesses and founders who need a reliable technical partner — someone who asks the right questions, builds with care, and delivers something that actually works.
